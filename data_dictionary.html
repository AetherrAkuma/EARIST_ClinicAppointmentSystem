Referential Integrity & Clinic Operating Constraints

  • Foreign Key Enforcement: SQLite PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON; is initialized on every database connection.
  • Cascading Deletes: Deleting a medical practitioner cascades to all schedules, availability overrides, and appointments; deleting an appointment purges corresponding outbox notifications.
  • Globally Unique Tracking ID: reference_code in appointments is enforced unique; generated as EAR-XXXX-XXX ensuring conflict-free patient process tracking.
  • Capacity Throttling: Maximum capacity of 5 patients per consultation slot (MAX_SLOT_CAPACITY = 5) ensures high clinical care standards and eliminates queue congestion.
  • Duplicate Booking Safeguard: Patients are prevented from scheduling overlapping appointments on identical dates and time windows.
